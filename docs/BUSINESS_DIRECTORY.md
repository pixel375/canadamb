# Business directory

Search active business profiles by name and category, with a Manitoba location filter on CanadaMB. Local portals include only businesses with a branch in that location. Owner submissions use a moderated queue and an exact location assignment. A verified account can submit a claim for an unclaimed profile. A separate administrator reviews the evidence independently and records a decision. Approval grants the verified claimant the owner role atomically and supersedes competing pending claims. Claimants can withdraw pending claims; reviewers may neither inspect nor decide their own claims or claims concerning businesses they manage. Evidence and the private claim email stay out of the public profile.

Authorized owners, managers and editors can edit descriptive and contact fields. They can submit announcements, promotions or updates for a branch they manage. Approved updates appear on eligible homepages and in the shared updates feed. A business can have several branch records. Team administration is owner-controlled. A default-off, review-staged access-request workflow adds seven-day recipient-accepted invitations and ownership transfers with password confirmation and independent network-admin review. Transfer requires a sole current owner, revokes the former owner's membership, and cancels outstanding requests. No email delivery is required. A separate review-staged ownership dispute intake supports private submissions, withdrawal, independent closure or escalation and claimant status. Escalated disputes remain in the review queue, block duplicate submissions and can be closed independently or withdrawn by the claimant. Invitations display participant account names and references without email addresses. Dispute review never changes memberships; contested ownership adjudication and legal evidence retention remain separate work. Review-staged branch additions and same-community address corrections preserve branch IDs; public contact email verification requires independent evidence, is invalidated when the email changes, and has no public badge or automated delivery. Owner-only independently reviewed branch closure and inter-community relocation preserve closed branch IDs and withdraw open jobs and announcements. Closed branches are excluded from public directory, jobs, updates and new commercial eligibility; reopening requires independent owner-requested review and never republishes withdrawn content. Automated contact challenges remain outstanding. Activate only after reviewed migration and retention approval; when the flag is enabled the old direct-grant endpoint is blocked.
