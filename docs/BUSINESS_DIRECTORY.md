# Business directory

Search active business profiles by name and category, with a Manitoba location filter on CanadaMB. Local portals include only businesses with a branch in that location. Owner submissions use a moderated queue and an exact location assignment. A verified account can submit a claim for an unclaimed profile. A separate administrator reviews the evidence independently and records a decision. Approval grants the claimant the owner role atomically. Evidence and the private claim email stay out of the public profile.

Authorized owners, managers and editors can edit descriptive and contact fields. They can submit announcements, promotions or updates for a branch they manage. Approved updates appear on eligible homepages and in the shared updates feed. A business can have several branch records. Team administration is owner-controlled. A default-off, review-staged access-request workflow adds seven-day recipient-accepted invitations and ownership transfers with password confirmation and independent network-admin review. Transfer requires a sole current owner, revokes the former owner's membership, and cancels outstanding requests. No email delivery is required. A separate review-staged ownership dispute intake supports private submissions, withdrawal, independent closure or escalation and claimant status. It never changes memberships; contested ownership adjudication, contact verification, branch editing and legal evidence retention remain separate work. Activate only after reviewed migration and retention approval; when the flag is enabled the old direct-grant endpoint is blocked.
