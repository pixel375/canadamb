Retain prelaunch access, registration, indexing, secrets, backup, reviewed database/flag activation gates and isolated automated CI tests. **Owner retention instruction (2026-11-27, mandatory):** Keep at most two complete retained Hostinger releases (active + one verified rollback) and two checksum-verified database/media backup sets after each successful audited update. Verify counts in the retention receipt; investigate failures before another deployment.
